Privacy Policy for Uni Spending Tracker

Last updated: 10 October 2026

1. Who operates the application

Uni Spending Tracker is a private, non-commercial personal budgeting application operated by a private individual in the United Kingdom ("I", "me" or "the operator"). No personal name or email address is published on this website because the application is used only by its operator and is not offered to other users.

2. Scope

The application is built for my own use. It is not offered to the public and is not intended to access anyone else's bank accounts or financial information. This notice explains how information is handled when I connect my own account and use the application.

The public GitHub Pages website hosting this policy and the Terms of Use contains no budgeting functionality and is not intended to receive banking data.

3. Information handled

Depending on the information made available by Nationwide Building Society through Enable Banking, the application may handle:

  • account information, such as account identifiers, account type, currency and balance;
  • transaction information, such as dates, amounts, currency, merchant or counterparty information and payment references;
  • categories, notes or other budgeting information added within the application;
  • Open Banking connection information, such as consent status, connection status and identifiers needed to retrieve account information; and
  • limited technical records needed to operate, troubleshoot and secure the application.

I do not intend to collect or store online-banking passwords or security credentials. Bank authentication takes place through the bank and the Open Banking flow provided by Enable Banking.

4. Where information comes from

Information comes from:

  • me, when I enter information or connect an account;
  • Nationwide Building Society, after I authorise access; and
  • Enable Banking, which provides the Open Banking connection.

5. How and why information is used

Information is used only to:

  • show my balances and transactions;
  • categorise and analyse my personal spending;
  • support personal budgeting; and
  • operate, maintain and troubleshoot the application.

The application is not used to make decisions about other people. It does not sell financial information, use it for advertising or provide it to data brokers.

Access to account information occurs only after authorisation through the Open Banking flow. I can withdraw that authorisation through the relevant bank or Enable Banking consent-management process. Because this is a purely personal application processing my own information, the UK GDPR's personal or household activity exemption may apply. This notice does not claim a legal status or lawful basis that has not been established. If the application's scope changes beyond personal use, this notice and the legal basis for processing will be reviewed before that change.

6. Service providers and sharing

Enable Banking processes information needed to provide the Open Banking connection. Nationwide Building Society processes information as the account provider. Each operates under its own terms and privacy information.

If private hosting, database or infrastructure services are enabled for the application, those providers may process information only as needed to supply their services. Their identity, processing location and contractual terms depend on the services actually selected. No financial data, API credentials or private keys are intentionally placed in this public GitHub repository or on the GitHub Pages website.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law. It is not otherwise intentionally shared with third parties.

7. International processing

Some service providers may process information outside the United Kingdom. The locations and safeguards depend on the providers actually selected. No claim is made here that a particular transfer mechanism applies where the application's deployment has not yet been finalised.

8. Retention and deletion

No fixed retention period is promised. Information is kept only while it is useful for my personal budgeting or reasonably needed to operate, troubleshoot or secure the application. It may be deleted when it is no longer needed, when the relevant account connection is removed, or when the application is retired. Copies may remain temporarily in backups or service-provider systems according to the applicable provider's arrangements.

9. Security

I take reasonable steps appropriate to a private personal project to limit access to the application and its data. API credentials, private keys and banking data are intended to remain private and are not intentionally committed to the public legal-pages repository. No internet-connected system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Choices and rights

I can review, correct or delete information in the application or its private storage, subject to the application's available controls. I can also revoke an Open Banking consent through Nationwide Building Society or Enable Banking.

Where data-protection law applies, individuals may have rights in relation to their personal data and may be able to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office. This statement does not suggest that information belonging to anyone other than the operator is intended to be processed.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the application, its providers or its data practices change. The updated version will be published on this website with a revised "Last updated" date.
